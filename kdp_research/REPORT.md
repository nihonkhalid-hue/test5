# REPORT — What sells on Amazon KDP (research of 2026-11-05)

_All tables below are generated by `scripts/report.py` from `master.csv` and the analysis CSVs. Every estimate is marked `_est` and its formula is in COVERAGE.md. Read the caveats before acting on any number._

## How to read this

- **Main Amazon source = profitable.app**, a third-party KDP dataset (547,892 Amazon books, snapshot "Updated Oct 9, 2026"). Its free page 1 (25 books) was read for ~280 Amazon categories × up to 4 lenses (all / new-and-trending / top-KDP / hidden-gems). Every book row carries the ASIN, format, publisher, publication date, print length, every Best-Sellers-Rank category placement, overall BSR, a "likely KDP" flag, Kindle Unlimited flag, price, rating and rating count, all copied verbatim.
- **Amazon direct**: 750 rows from 15 Best Sellers pages (amazon.com root + 12 categories incl. Coloring Books for Grown-Ups, amazon.co.uk root, amazon.de root) before CAPTCHAs stopped every marketplace. No product (dp) pages, so trim size is almost never known.
- **ASINsight**: Amazon keyword reports (top 10 per keyword with a third-party "Units Sold/mo" estimate; report month January 2026). For book keywords these lists are dominated by supplies (pens, markers, planners from stationery brands); rows are kept and flagged `is_book`.
- **Sales numbers**: `monthly_sales_est_bsr` uses the TCK Publishing BSR-to-sales model on the overall BSR. All profitable.app books are bestsellers in at least one category, so **every median in this report describes books that are already selling**, not the average new listing (survivorship bias). Use the medians to compare niches against each other, not as an income promise.
- "Indie" = publisher field says "Independently published" **or** profitable.app flags the book as likely-KDP. Many KDP authors use an imprint name, so the exact "Independently published" share understates indie presence.

## Executive summary

1. **Romance on Kindle is the biggest indie money pool** (median ~1,000 est. sales/month per bestseller, 40–85% indie depending on sub-niche, 71% in Kindle Unlimited, Google Trends "romantasy" +52% y/y US). Billionaire, dark/mafia, monster/sci-fi, small-town/cowboy and hockey romance top the opportunity table. It is a writing business (series of 300–450-page novels, fast release cadence), not a low-content one.
2. **For low/medium-content books, coloring is still the deepest indie-friendly market**: 1,152 bestseller listings, 59% indie, $9.99 is the standard price (the KDP 60% royalty threshold), ~100 pages, ~$3.15 royalty per sale on an 8.5×11 paperback. Cozy coloring (Coco Wyo style) is the strongest coloring sub-niche; bold-and-easy is large but its Google Trends signal dropped 16% y/y in the US.
3. **Puzzle books are mature**: 837 listings, 53% indie, but "word search" interest is -13% y/y (US) while "sudoku book" (+74%) and "puzzle book" (+96%) rose. Crossword is the weakest puzzle niche (median 10 est. sales/mo). Themed puzzle books (murder-mystery sudoku, Bible word search, sports-team kids books, seasonal "would you rather") are the fastest-rising puzzle titles.
4. **Journals and planners are dominated by brands**: dated planners 3% indie with the top 3 sellers holding 92% of estimated sales; wall calendars are traditional publishers (BrownTrout, Andrews McMeel). Only gratitude and shadow-work/healing journals show indie room, and gratitude-journal search interest had a one-off spike in late 2025–spring 2026 that has already faded (last 13 weeks back near the year-ago level).
5. **How-to-draw anime/manga/kawaii** is the best-fitting niche for this audience: 52% indie, $13.49 median, ~129 pages, rising Google Trends ("how to draw anime" +95% y/y US), but the median bestseller sells only ~48/month and Christopher Hart plus a few series (Shinjuku Press, Draw Like a Mangaka) take most sales. Kawaii/creepy-cute/K-pop coloring books by indies (Coco Wyo, Cecine Valin, Lumi Lark, Leriza May) are the fastest-rising Japan-adjacent titles.
6. **Price**: the best monthly royalty per bestseller comes from $12.99–$19.99 paperbacks in coloring, puzzle and workbooks (royalty per sale $4.95–$7.35); under $9.99 a paperback earns only 50% of list price minus printing, which leaves $0.16–$1.16 per sale.

## Recommendations for this seller (anime / manga / gaming / kawaii / cozy Japan / retro / J-pop, mostly women)

1. **Start with original-character kawaii/cozy-Japan coloring books on KDP** (8.5×11 paperback, 80–110 single-sided pages, $9.99–$12.99). Evidence: indie share 82% in anime/kawaii coloring and 67% in cozy coloring; the top velocity titles in the Japan section are indie kawaii/K-pop/creepy-cute coloring books published within the last year. Reuse the digital coloring pages already sold on the website, which are the same asset in two channels. Publish Halloween/creepy-kawaii editions by mid-August and Christmas/winter editions by mid-October (Etsy RankHero peaks: Halloween coloring Oct, Christmas coloring Dec; Google Trends "coloring book" peaks in December).
2. **Second product: a "How to draw kawaii / chibi / anime characters" workbook** (paperback 120–150 pages, $12.99–$16.99, plus a $4.99 Kindle edition). Prices above $12.99 lift the royalty per sale above $4.95 with no visible loss of sales in the art-instruction price bands. Publish by mid-December; "how to draw anime" peaks in February (New Year's resolutions and art gifts).
3. **Third product: a kawaii/anime-style "creepy cute" or "cozy Japan" themed puzzle book** (sudoku or logic puzzles plus coloring inserts; 100–150 pages at $10.99–$14.99), since sudoku and mixed-puzzle niches are rising and themed puzzle books are the fastest-rising puzzle titles. Avoid plain large-print word search (-13% y/y and dominated by Brain Games/Puzzlewright).
4. **Keep everything original**: no Sanrio, Pokémon, Ghibli, Demon Slayer, Genshin or K-pop group names in titles, covers or keywords (see IP-risk flags). Describe the style ("kawaii", "chibi", "anime-style", "cozy Japan", "pastel goth") instead.
5. **Do not start with dated planners, wall calendars, blank notebooks, password books, prayer journals or sticker books** (see the do-NOT-enter list): brand-dominated, <15% indie, or no newcomer breaking through.
6. **Romance is the largest indie opportunity overall, but only if the seller wants to write fiction.** If so, pick a trope with high indie share and KU (billionaire, monster/sci-fi, small-town) and plan a series.

## Caveats

- Survivorship: profitable.app and Amazon Best Sellers only show books that already sell; the "newcomer rate" is high because unsuccessful new books are invisible. It is still useful for comparing niches.
- BSR→sales uses one published model (TCK Publishing) for all categories; real sales per BSR vary by season and category. ASINsight units are a different third-party estimate (January 2026). profitable.app's `monthlyRevenue` is kept as `monthly_revenue_est_thirdparty` and not mixed with ours.
- Royalties are what a KDP edition **would** earn at that list price and page count (constants in COVERAGE.md). Most traditional-publisher titles are not on KDP.
- Trim size is mostly NOT SHOWN (Amazon product pages were blocked); royalty assumes large trim (8.5×11) for coloring, puzzle, activity, workbook, art and picture books and regular trim (6×9) otherwise.
- Google Trends y/y figures for low-volume terms ("low content books", "gratitude journal") are noisy; check `last13w_vs_year_ago_pct` in seasonality.csv as well as the 52-week figure.
