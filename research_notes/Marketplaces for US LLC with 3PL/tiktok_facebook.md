## Q1. TikTok Shop US: can a non-US-resident owner with an ITIN pass verification? Requirements, cross-border program, fees, fulfillment, affiliates, ITIN timing

### Takeaway
Probably not as the sole person. TikTok Shop US's published rules say sellers must reside in the shop's country. The Primary Business Representative needs a US residential address and a US government ID (US passport, driver's license or state ID). The SSN/ITIN last-4 field for that person is also reported. A foreign passport is reported to be accepted only for beneficial owners (25% or more), not for the representative. So an ITIN alone does not fix the residency and US-ID requirement. The workable route that vendors describe is to appoint a real US person as representative, which carries legal and tax risk. TikTok's cross-border program for the US is aimed at China/HK-licensed entities with US warehouses. I found no evidence that Morocco is an eligible origin country.

**ITIN**
- General rule: an ITIN is issued only with an attached federal tax return unless an exception applies. Exception 1 covers passive income with third-party withholding or treaty benefits, and 1(d) requires a letter from the withholding agent. Exception 2 covers wages, scholarships, gambling and similar income. — [IRS ITIN FAQ](https://irs.gov/tin/itin/itin-application-frequently-asked-questions); [IRS Pub 1915](https://www.irs.gov/pub1915); [teachmepersonalfinance.com](https://www.teachmepersonalfinance.com/irs-form-w-7-instructions/)
- Processing takes 7–11 weeks normally, up to 14–16 weeks in Jan–Apr peak, and 9–11 weeks from abroad. A Certified Acceptance Agent (CAA) verifies the passport so you keep the original, but does not speed up IRS processing. — [easyfiling.com](https://easyfiling.com/blog/irs-itin-processing-time/); [businessglobalizer.com](https://businessglobalizer.com/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-get-an-itin-a-step-by-step-process-turn-around-time/); [visaverge.com](https://www.visaverge.com/documentation/itin-application-guide-2026-step-by-step-with-form-w-7/)

### Inferences
- An ITIN only meets the tax-number field. It does not meet the "reside in the US," US residential address or US government ID requirements for the Primary Business Representative. A Moroccan resident with an ITIN therefore likely still cannot serve as representative. They can probably be listed as the UBO using a Moroccan passport.
- Appointing a US "nominee" representative is the common workaround. It exposes the owner to account-control and legal risk, may misrepresent who runs the business, and cannot be changed later. Logging in from Morocco may add risk flags.
- Morocco does not appear as a TikTok Shop market or as a US cross-border origin.
- A typical sole owner of an e-commerce LLC probably has no ITIN exception. They would get an ITIN by filing a return (for example, Form 1040-NR if there is effectively connected income). Note that a foreign-owned single-member LLC files Form 5472 with a pro-forma 1120, which is not the owner's own return.

- The current US referral rate (6% vs 8%) is unresolved.

## Q2. Facebook Marketplace / Meta in 2025–2026: can a business sell shipped products in the US? Checkout, eBay partnership, Instagram tags, account requirements, Morocco login, Groups

### Takeaway
Meta removed native checkout from Facebook and Instagram Shops between June and Sept 2025. Shops and Instagram product tags now send buyers to the seller's own website. Marketplace shipping still exists, but it is mainly a person-to-person feature on personal profiles. Its fees, label access and business eligibility are inconsistent. eBay listings do appear on Facebook Marketplace in the US, but eBay chooses which ones based on trends and listing quality, and sellers cannot opt in. The practical route for a US LLC is to list on eBay and possibly be surfaced on Marketplace, plus run Shops and Instagram tags that link to its own site.

### Cited Findings
**Checkout and Shops**
- Meta began phasing out native checkout on Facebook and Instagram Shops in June 2025, aiming to finish by Aug 2025. Sources give the end date as Aug 4 or as Sept 2025. Buyers now check out on the seller's own website, and Meta no longer handles order management or disputes. — [Feedonomics](https://feedonomics.com/blog/meta-removing-native-checkout/); [GoDataFeed](https://www.godatafeed.com/blog/meta-is-dropping-native-checkout-on-facebook-and-instagram); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/meta-ditches-onsite-checkout-on-facebook-and-instagram/); [BigCommerce](https://www.bigcommerce.com/blog/updates-to-meta-shops-checkout-for-bigcommerce/)
- Instagram product tags now open the product page on the merchant's website. Requirements include a Business or Creator account, a linked Facebook Page and Commerce Manager. The old "checkout with Facebook/Instagram" option was US-business-only. — [Agorapulse support](https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/8769612-setting-up-commerce-manager-for-product-tagging-on-instagram); [whatmore.ai, Aug 2026 (VENDOR)](https://www.whatmore.ai/blog/why-meta-s-instagram-product-tagging-update-matters-and-why-you-still-need-your-own-shoppable-video-storefront/); [BigCommerce support](https://support.bigcommerce.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Sell-on-Instagram)

**Groups**
- Buy/sell groups are moderated and each has its own rules, often including bans on business or reseller posts. Common conventions are SSTP (sold subject to payment), PPU (pending pick up) and first-to-pay. — [groupboss.io (VENDOR tool)](https://groupboss.io/blog/buy-and-sell-facebook-groups/); [getonecart.com](https://www.getonecart.com/how-to-sell-on-facebook/); [underpriced.app](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/facebook-groups-vs-marketplace-2026)

### Inferences
- Selling shipped products on Marketplace under its own checkout is unreliable for a business entity. The steadier routes are eBay listings that may get surfaced on Marketplace (not guaranteed or controllable), Shops and Instagram tags pointing to a Shopify-style site, and Meta ads. Fulfillment from a Texas 3PL works with all of these.
- A personal Marketplace profile based in Morocco would likely not be able to offer US shipping or receive payouts, since US bank or identity details are expected. This is inferred, not confirmed.
- Website-checkout Shops have no in-app payout, so the owner's location matters less there. Commerce Manager mainly needs a Page, a catalog and a domain.
- Groups suit community and flash-deal promotion that drives traffic to the store or eBay listing. Read each group's rules first.
