## 1. End of US de minimis (Section 321): timeline and status in Oct 2026

### Takeaway
There is no $800 duty-free de minimis for any country. It was suspended for all countries from Aug 29, 2025 (EO 14324) and continued by EO 14388 (Feb 2026). CBP made the suspension indefinite by interim final rules on June 24, 2026, and the statute (OBBBA) repeals it permanently on July 1, 2027. Every parcel from Morocco now needs a formal or informal entry and pays all applicable duties.

### Cited Findings
- EO 14324 suspended duty-free de minimis treatment for low-value imports from all countries effective Aug 29, 2025. — [GHY](https://www.ghy.com/trade-compliance/us-to-end-de-minimis-exemption-for-low-value-imports-on-august-29/); [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1355662653395)
- After the Supreme Court IEEPA ruling, the February 2026 order EO 14388 continued the suspension under 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C), and CBP is to keep collecting duties, taxes and fees on these shipments (CSMS #67845486). — [Allied CHB](https://alliedchb.com/news/csms-67845486-continuing-the-suspension-of-duty-free-de-minimis-treatment-for-all)
- June 24, 2026: CBP published two interim final rules. (1) Indefinite suspension for shipments of $800 or less arriving by any mode other than the international postal network, effective June 24, 2026 (FR doc 2026-12670). (2) Indefinite suspension for mail, plus a new "postal informal entry" process (FR doc 2026-12669, 91 FR 37801). Comments were due July 24, 2026. — [Federal Register 2026-12670](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12670/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-merchandise-arriving-through-all-modes-other); [Federal Register 2026-12669](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12669/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-mail-shipments-and-new-postal-informal-entry); [Thompson Hine](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/06/cbp-issues-interim-final-rules-indefinitely-suspending-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-imports/); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/06/cbp-suspends-de-minimis-exemption.html)
- The postal rule takes effect July 24, 2026 (one section, 19 CFR 145.31, took effect June 24). One source cites a delayed compliance date of **Oct 22, 2026** for certain specified shipments. The process covers mail merchandise valued at up to $2,500. — [Diaz Trade Law](https://diaztradelaw.com/postal-de-minimis-rule/); [Expeditors](https://info.expeditors.com/newsflash/cbp-issues-guidance-for-international-mail-shipments)
- Postal carriers and CBP-certified "qualified parties" collecting duties on mail may use the ad valorem duty method. The flat per-item amounts used in 2025 were tied to IEEPA and, per this source, are no longer the mechanism. Only one source confirms this. — [Expeditors](https://info.expeditors.com/newsflash/cbp-issues-guidance-for-international-mail-shipments)
- The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-21, §70531) statutorily eliminates de minimis effective July 1, 2027. — search summary of [Federal Register 2026-12670](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12670/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-merchandise-arriving-through-all-modes-other); [Velaxis](https://velaxisglobal.com/blog/de-minimis-permanent-suspension-2026.html)
- In 2025, many foreign posts (Japan Post and others) suspended EMS to the US after Aug 29, 2025. Etsy suspended US-bound label purchases for Australia Post, Canada Post and Evri. — [InterContinental Osaka notice](https://icosaka.com/en/ems/); [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1355662653395)

### Inferences
- For a Moroccan seller, no shipment value is low enough to avoid an entry and duties. Even a $30 ceramic bowl owes MFN duty plus the 12.5% Section 301 duty (see section 2) plus carrier or broker fees.
- With de minimis gone, the main benefit of the US LLC is that it can act as importer of record (IOR) for bulk shipments. One formal entry per pallet or box spreads broker fees across many items, compared with one entry per parcel.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Barid Al-Maghrib / Amana (Morocco Post) EMS currently accepts US-bound parcels or has a CBP-certified duty-collection arrangement. No Morocco-specific postal notice was found. Assume it is unreliable or unavailable until Amana confirms in writing.
- The exact list of shipments subject to the Oct 22, 2026 delayed compliance date was not retrieved.

## 2. Tariffs and duties on Moroccan goods in Oct 2026 (reciprocal or Section 301, MFN, US-Morocco FTA)

### Takeaway
Through 2025, Morocco paid the 10% IEEPA "reciprocal" baseline. The Supreme Court struck those tariffs down on Feb 20, 2026. A 10% Section 122 surcharge applied from Feb 24 to July 24, 2026. Since July 24, 2026, Moroccan goods carry a **12.5% Section 301 "forced-labor" tariff** (HTS 9903.05.56), stacked on top of MFN duty. The US-Morocco FTA can remove the MFN duty for qualifying goods, but according to the one source that addresses it, it does **not** exempt goods from the Section 301 add-on.

### Cited Findings
- **Older info, 2025:** Morocco was in the 10% IEEPA baseline reciprocal tier. On Feb 20, 2026, the Supreme Court (V.O.S. Selections v. US, 6-3) held that IEEPA does not authorize tariffs, which invalidated all IEEPA reciprocal tariffs. — [Flexport](https://www.flexport.com/blog/the-supreme-courts-ieepa-tariff-ruling-next-steps-potential-refunds-and/); [BDO](https://www.bdo.com/insights/tax/supreme-court-invalidates-ieepa-tariffs-administration-replaces-with-new-surcharge-what-importers)
- **Older info, Feb to July 2026:** A 10% Section 122 surcharge applied to all countries from Feb 24, 2026 and expired July 24, 2026 (150-day cap). The Court of International Trade ruled it invalid, and that decision is on appeal. — [Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/alert-Trump-Imposes-Section-122-Tariffs-After-Halting-IEEPA-Tariffs-Previews-New-Section-301-Investigations); [Ward and Smith](https://www.wardandsmith.com/article/court-of-international-trade-rejects-10-section-122-tariff-what-businesses-should-know-while-the-appeal-proceeds)
- **Current:** USTR imposed Section 301 forced-labor tariffs on about 60 economies, effective 12:01 a.m. ET July 24, 2026 (cited as 91 FR 47318). There are two tiers, 10% and 12.5%. — [Honigman](https://www.honigman.com/alert-3462); [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/07/and-the-tariff-beat-goes-on); [Baker Donelson](https://www.bakerdonelson.com/forced-labor-section-301-tariffs-imposed-to-replace-expiring-section-122-tariffs-what-importers-need-to-know)
- **Morocco's rate is 12.5%** (HTS 9903.05.56), applied across virtually all Moroccan exports. Exceptions are headings 9903.05.85–9903.05.92 (in-transit goods were covered by 9903.05.85 until July 27, 2026) and goods already under Section 232. The duty stacks on MFN. The rate was first proposed in June 2026. — [AACB](https://www.aacb.com/trade-tariff-news/section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-on-60-economies); [Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/06/314926/us-proposes-12-5-tariff-on-morocco-over-forced-labor-import-allegations/); [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/morocco-12-5-percent-us-tariff-forced-labour-imports-2026/); [Swap Commerce](https://help.swap-commerce.com/en/articles/16069139-us-tariff-update-24-july-2026-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-on-imports-from-60-economies)
- **FTA does not exempt goods from Section 301:** The only FTA-related exemptions are USMCA-qualifying goods and CAFTA-DR and Jordan FTA textiles. The US-Morocco FTA is not separately exempted. Only one source (Zonos) says this explicitly. — [Zonos Section 301 guide](https://zonos.com/docs/guides/country-guides/united-states/section-301-forced-labor)
- **MFN rates for rugs (current HTS):** Hand-knotted wool carpets (5701.10.90) are 4.5%. Hand-hooked wool (5701.10.40) is Free. Hand-knotted rugs of other textile materials (5701.90.10) are Free. Older CBP rulings citing 5.1%, 4.1% and 2.6% are outdated. — [USITC HTS ch. 57](https://usitc.gov/publications/docs/tata/hts/bychapter/0500c57.pdf); [US Tariff Rates – Carpets](https://ustariffrates.com/chapter/carpets); [Wove](https://tariffs.wove.com/us/tariff/5701.10.90.00)
- **FTA mechanics:** CBP administers the US-Morocco FTA (in force 2006). Rules of origin are in HTSUS General Note 27 and 19 CFR Part 102. The implementing regulations are 19 CFR Part 10, Subpart M (§§10.761–10.786). — [eCFR / Cornell 19 CFR 10 Subpart M](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/19/part-10/subpart-M); [CBP Morocco FTA FAQ](https://cbp.gov/trade/free-trade-agreements/morocco/faqs)
- **How to claim:** The importer claims at entry by prefixing the HTSUS number with the Special Program Indicator "MA" on the entry summary or by EDI (19 CFR 10.762). Sources conflict: CBP's FAQ page reportedly shows "BH", which looks like an error. A preference not claimed at entry can be claimed by PSC/PEA within one year. Post-importation 1520(d) claims are **not** available. The importer attests to eligibility and must provide a declaration about production on request (19 CFR 10.764). — [19 CFR 10.762 (GPO)](https://www.gpo.gov/fdsys/pkg/CFR-2014-title19-vol1/pdf/CFR-2014-title19-vol1-sec10-762.pdf); [CBP Morocco FTA FAQ](https://cbp.gov/trade/free-trade-agreements/morocco/faqs)
- Under the FTA, duties on qualifying textile, apparel, footwear and travel goods (which covers leather handbags and flat goods) have been eliminated. Textiles are subject to FTA origin rules (yarn-forward). — [ITA summary of Morocco FTA textiles](https://trade.gov/summary-morocco-fta-textiles); [USTR textile fact sheet (2004 archive, older)](https://ustr.gov/archive/Document_Library/Fact_Sheets/2004/US_-_Morocco_Free_Trade_Agreement_Textile_Apparel_Provisions.html)
- Unrelated Morocco carve-out: on June 29, 2026, the US suspended AD/CVD duties on Moroccan phosphate fertilizer for 8 months. This does not affect consumer goods. — [Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2845779-trump-suspends-morocco-phosphate-duties-for-8-months)

### Inferences
- Rough landed duty on a hand-knotted Moroccan wool rug (5701.10.90) declared at $500:
  - FTA claimed correctly: MFN 0% + 12.5% Section 301 = about $62.50, plus MPF and carrier fees.
  - FTA not claimed: 4.5% + 12.5% = about $85.
- Claiming "MA" therefore still saves the MFN part (up to about 4.5% on rugs, and more on leather and ceramics lines with higher MFN rates). The Section 301 12.5% is the larger cost and cannot be avoided.
- Handmade Berber rugs woven from Moroccan wool, Moroccan leather poufs and Moroccan pottery would generally be "wholly obtained or produced" in Morocco, which is the strongest origin case. Goods using imported yarn or synthetic fibers need a yarn-forward analysis. *This is inferred, not verified for specific products.*
- Express couriers (DHL/FedEx/UPS) clearing parcels by informal entry often do not claim FTA preference by default. The seller should put "Country of origin: Morocco – claim US-Morocco FTA (SPI MA)" plus the HTS code on the commercial invoice and confirm the broker applies it. *Inferred from how claims work; unverified carrier practice.*

### Gaps
- Current exact MFN rates for leather poufs (likely 4202 or 9404, depending on stuffing and classification), leather bags (4202), ceramic tableware (6912) and decorative ceramics (6913), and cotton or wool textiles (6302, 6304) were not retrieved. Check them in the [USITC HTS search](https://hts.usitc.gov).
- Whether the 9903.05.85–9903.06.21 exemption headings cover any handicraft or "handmade" category is unknown. No handicraft carve-out was found.
- Status of the Section 122 appeal and any refunds of IEEPA or Section 122 duties paid by small importers was not covered.
- Whether the Section 301 duty applies to postal shipments at the same ad valorem rate was not confirmed.

## 3. Importer of record via the US LLC: CBP Form 5106, bonds, brokers, ACE, DDP vs DDU, Etsy/Shopify

### Takeaway
The US LLC (with an EIN) can be the IOR by filing CBP Form 5106, usually through a licensed customs broker. Shipments over $2,500 (formal entry) need a customs bond, either single-entry or continuous. A June 2026 executive order (EO 14411) will tighten bond and vetting rules for foreign-controlled IORs, but as of Sept 2026 CBP had not issued the implementing rules. Since **July 9, 2026, Etsy requires DDP for orders to US buyers from non-US sellers**, or the seller loses Purchase Protection and pays any buyer-charged duties.

### Cited Findings
- Form 5106 creates or updates the importer identity record. A US LLC uses its EIN. A foreign entity without an EIN gets a CBP-Assigned Importer Number (CAIN). — [Greenwich Mercantile – Form 5106 guide](https://www.greenwich-mercantile.com/resources/guides/cbp-form-5106); [Greenwich Mercantile – Non-resident importer](https://www.greenwich-mercantile.com/resources/guides/non-resident-importer); [usacustomsclearance.com](https://usacustomsclearance.com/process/complete-guide-to-cbp-form-5106/)
- **New in 2026:** From Sept 18, 2026, CBP voids importer numbers whose Form 5106 data is inaccurate or incomplete. A continuous bond written against a voided record becomes ineffective until the number is restored. — [GoCubic](https://www.gocubic.io/blog/cbp-void-importer-number-form-5106-2026); [MoreShores](https://moreshores.com/media/importer-of-record-requirements)
- **New in 2026:** Executive Order 14411 (June 3, 2026) directs CBP to raise minimum bond amounts and to limit foreign importers' use of continuous bonds. Once the rules are issued, foreign IORs would need to file through a CTPAT-validated licensed broker. As of Sept 2026, CBP had not issued these rules. — [Carra Globe](https://carraglobe.com/customs-enforcement-executive-order-ior-2026/); [Greenwich Mercantile](https://www.greenwich-mercantile.com/resources/guides/cbp-form-5106)
- No source confirmed a specific new minimum bond figure (the commonly cited $50,000 figure is the long-standing continuous-bond minimum, not a new 2026 rule). — same sources (gap)
- Postal and low-value informal entries cover goods up to $2,500. — [Diaz Trade Law](https://diaztradelaw.com/postal-de-minimis-rule/)
- **Etsy DDP mandate:** announced June 9, 2026, effective July 9, 2026.
  - Non-US sellers shipping to US buyers must ship DDP and build duties into item or shipping price.
  - Non-DDP orders lose Purchase Protection. If the buyer is charged duties, the buyer is refunded and the amount is deducted from the seller.
  - Etsy offers a beta US tariff estimator in the listing editor and names DDP-capable carriers (UPS, FedEx).
  - Fallback where DDP is unavailable: disclose duties in the listing and obtain the buyer's written agreement before shipping.
  - Sources: [Etsy community announcement](https://community.etsy.com/forum/announcements-290/topic/upcoming-policy-changes-for-orders-shipped-to-the-us-183836/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-requires-ddp-shipping-us-tariffs/); [Value Added Resource – estimator](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-tests-us-tariff-calculator/); [Swotzy](https://www.swotzy.com/shipping-insights/etsy-ddp-shipping-to-the-us-what-changes-on-july-9)
- DDU produces surprise collection bills for buyers, which is why sellers prefer DDP. UPS and DHL support both. — [eBay community thread](https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/eBay-should-add-option-for-DDP-shipping-de-minimis-removed/m-p/35299904/highlight/true); [eBay export tariff update](https://export.ebay.com/en/resources/important-updates/us-tariffs)
- DHL's 2026 US rate guide lists a duty-tax-paid (DTP/DDP) service fee of 2% of duties, minimum $17. DHL rates rose 5.9% on Jan 1, 2026. — [DHL Service & Rate Guide 2026 (US)](https://www.dhl.com/content/dam/dhl/global/dhl-express/documents/pdf/service-and-rate-guide-us-en.pdf); [Shipwave](https://shipwave.app/ship/dhl-shipping-calculator)

### Inferences
- If the Moroccan seller ships direct-to-consumer from Morocco on Etsy, DDP through DHL Express or UPS with a Moroccan business account billing duties to the shipper is now effectively mandatory. The US LLC does not need to be IOR for each parcel because the courier's broker handles the informal entry.
- The US LLC as IOR pays off mainly for **bulk B2B imports into US inventory**: one formal entry with a single-entry or continuous bond and a broker. Each later domestic sale then carries no per-parcel duty or brokerage.
- With EO 14411 pending, a foreign-owned US LLC should expect closer vetting of its 5106 record (beneficial owner data) and should make sure the 5106 data is complete before Sept 18 voiding sweeps affect it. Whether a US LLC with a foreign owner is treated as a "foreign importer" under EO 14411 is unclear.

### Gaps
- Typical 2026 customs broker fees for a small formal entry and the cost of single-entry vs continuous bond premiums were not retrieved. Continuous bonds historically cost a few hundred USD per year for the $50k minimum (older info, unverified).
- How Shopify Managed Markets / Shopify Markets collects US duties for sellers shipping from Morocco was not found.
- ACE portal self-filing requirements for a small LLC were not researched.

## 4. International carriers from Morocco: DHL / Aramex / FedEx / UPS discounts, consolidators, Amana EMS

### Takeaway
Little reliable, published Morocco-to-US rate data exists. Express couriers (DHL Express in particular) are the default DDP-capable channel. Amana/EMS reliability for US-bound parcels after de minimis is unconfirmed. Volume discounts require a business account negotiated with the local sales office.

### Cited Findings
- DHL Morocco: clearance is included in its rates but duties and taxes are not. DTP shifts duties to the sender's business account. — [DHL Express Morocco](https://www.dhl.com/ma-en/home/express.html)
- For US imports, US Customs sets duty by contents and origin and can adjust it after entry. — [DHL US Rate Guide 2026](https://mydhl.express.dhl/content/dam/downloads/us/en/rate-guide/service_and_rate_guide_us_en_2026.pdf.coredownload.pdf)
- Third-party resellers such as ParcelABC advertise discounted DHL from Morocco to the US. — [ParcelABC](https://www.parcelabc.com/tag/dhl-shipping-from-morocco-to-united-states-with-parcelabc/20/232/c148)
- A Moroccan rug listing on eBay ships via DHL with the standard "subject to customs charges" note. — [eBay listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/135280756272)

### Inferences
- **Option A (direct DTC):** DHL Express DTP with a Moroccan business account. This is fast, but each parcel carries the express rate, duties of MFN plus 12.5%, and the DTP fee (2% of duties, $17 minimum).
- **Option B (bulk to US 3PL):** sea or air freight, with the US LLC as IOR, then domestic USPS/UPS. This becomes much cheaper per unit once volume exceeds a few dozen items per month, especially for heavy rugs and poufs.

### Gaps
- No published 2026 Morocco-to-US rates were found for DHL, Aramex, FedEx or UPS. Aramex and Shop&Ship pricing was not found.
- Amana EMS status for US shipments in 2026 is unknown.

## 5. Domestic US shipping discounts (Pirate Ship, ShipStation, Shippo, EasyPost, UPS Digital Access, Cubic)

### Takeaway
Pirate Ship gives free access to USPS Commercial pricing and discounted UPS, with no subscription, per-label fee or minimum. USPS changed Ground Advantage and Cubic rules on July 12, 2026, and a temporary 8% surcharge runs until Jan 17, 2027.

### Cited Findings
- Pirate Ship charges $0 (no subscription, per-label fee, markup or minimum) and offers USPS and UPS only. — [Ship-Audit review](https://ship-audit.com/pirate-ship-review/)
- USPS changes on July 12, 2026:
  - Ground Advantage moves to ounce-based pricing changes.
  - Cubic maximum length rises from 18" to 22".
  - Cubic Softpack pricing tiers change.
  - Pirate Ship says its below-Commercial contiguous rates keep ounce pricing under 1 lb.
  - Sources: [Pirate Ship support – July 2026 changes](https://support.pirateship.com/en/articles/15453569-july-2026-usps-rate-and-rule-changes); [Amazon Seller Forums](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/8e7b9e46-cac4-46a4-ac63-653f87027008)
- USPS added a temporary 8% surcharge on all domestic services from April 26, 2026 to Jan 17, 2027. — [Ship-Audit](https://ship-audit.com/pirate-ship-review/); [Sellers Ask Sellers](https://sellersasksellers.com/t/2026-first-postal-increase-of-the-year/6864)
- Example Ground Advantage rates for Zone 5, July 2026 (secondary source): 1 lb at $8.74 Commercial vs $10.95 retail; 5 lb at $13.48. — [Ship.com](https://www.ship.com/post/usps-ground-advantage-vs-priority-mail-2026)
- One reseller blog says UPS Ground via Pirate Ship often beats USPS at 3 lb and up. — [Flipsail](https://www.flipsail.io/blog/reseller-shipping-guide-2026)

### Inferences
- Heavy rugs (5–20 lb) are generally cheaper on UPS Ground via Pirate Ship than on USPS. Small ceramics or leather goods under 1 lb, or in small boxes, suit Ground Advantage or Cubic.

## 6. US 3PLs, Amazon MCF/FBA, mail-forwarding and virtual addresses

### Takeaway
Big 3PLs have minimums: ShipMonk about $250/month; ShipBob about $275/month plus roughly $975 setup (secondary sources, which conflict). Bulky rugs may cost more at Amazon. Virtual addresses range from about $5–40/month (iPostal1, Anytime Mailbox) to $49/month and up (Stable) and $229/month (Earth Class Mail Business MailRoom).

### Cited Findings
- **ShipMonk:** about $250/month minimum, no setup fee. Picks from $2.50 first item and $0.50 each additional. Pallet storage about $25/month. — [FitSmallBusiness](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/shipbob-vs-shipmonk/); [Warehousingcosts](https://warehousingcosts.com/guides/shipbob-vs-shipmonk)
- **ShipBob:** about $975 setup and $275 monthly minimum. Picks $2.50–3.50 per order. Pallet storage about $40/month. Shipping markup reportedly 15–30% plus a 3% card surcharge. Sources conflict, and some list a $40 minimum. — [Delivery Group](https://deliverygroupinc.com/blog/shipbob-pricing-2026); [Fulfill.com](https://www.fulfill.com/shipbob-vs-shipmonk); [ShipCalm](https://www.shipcalm.com/blog/shipbob-vs-shipmonk/)
- **Amazon MCF vs ShipBob:** per-unit cost is often similar, but Amazon adds size surcharges for oversized goods. — [Sellable](https://sellable.pro/comparison/shipbob-vs-amazon-mcf)
- **Virtual mailboxes:**
  - iPostal1: $9.99 / $14.99 / $24.99 / $39.99 plans.
  - Anytime Mailbox: from $4.99/month.
  - Stable: $49/month Grow plan; registered agent $25/month extra.
  - Earth Class Mail: $229/month Business MailRoom plan.
  - Sources: [PostScanMail](https://www.postscanmail.com/?p=8638); [Business Anywhere](https://businessanywhere.io/best-anytime-mailbox-alternatives/); [StartupOwl – Stable](https://startupowl.com/reviews/stable); [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/anytime-mailbox/pricing)

### Inferences
- At low volume, a small regional 3PL with no minimums, or a prep center, is likely a better fit for bulky handmade goods than ShipBob or ShipMonk. Amazon FBA is penalised for oversize rugs.
- A virtual mailbox gives the LLC a US address for 5106, bank and marketplace use. It is not a warehouse for inventory.

### Gaps
- No 2026 data was found for Amazon FBA/MCF fee tables, Shopify Fulfillment (Flexport, ex-Deliverr) status, or names and pricing of small 3PLs.
