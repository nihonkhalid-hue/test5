# Startup Credits, Software Perks, Business Accounts, Grants, Insurance and Phone Numbers for a Small Foreign-Owned US LLC (E-commerce + Blog), as of October 2026

Research method note: Most findings come from web search result summaries. Direct page fetches failed for several domains (mercury.com, techradar.com returned DNS errors in this environment), so many figures rely on third-party aggregators (guptadeepak.com, spendbase, superscout, builtfirst, ringly). These are flagged where relevant. Verify amounts on official pages before relying on them.

## Cloud/startup credit programs (AWS, Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare, DigitalOcean, AI labs, SaaS startup programs)

### Takeaway
A bootstrapped, non-VC-backed LLC can realistically self-serve only the entry tiers: AWS Activate Founders (~$1,000), Google for Startups Cloud "Start" (up to $2,000, 1 year), Microsoft for Startups self-service (up to ~$5,000 Azure), and Cloudflare for Startups "Bootstrapped" (~$5K). Larger tiers ($100K–$350K) need a VC/accelerator Org ID or referral. Microsoft's Founders Hub was cut sharply in mid-2025 (old $150K open program ended; OpenAI credits removed), and DigitalOcean Hatch has reportedly been replaced. For an e-commerce + blog business, these cloud credits matter little unless self-hosting.

### Cited Findings
**AWS Activate**
- Founders tier (self-serve): $1,000 in credits; "select startups may qualify for up to $5,000 over time." Eligibility: new to Activate credits, self-funded, fully functioning company website, founded in the past 10 years. AWS guide dated June 2026 — [AWS: Applying for Activate credits](https://aws.amazon.com/aws-startups/learn/applying-for-aws-activate-credits-a-step-by-step-guide/)
- Portfolio tier requires an Organization ID from an AWS Activate Provider (VC/accelerator/incubator); ceiling reported as $100K by one guide and up to $200K by AWS's guide (conflict) — [AWS guide](https://aws.amazon.com/aws-startups/learn/applying-for-aws-activate-credits-a-step-by-step-guide/); [guptadeepak AWS Activate](https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/guides/aws-activate)
- Third-party guides: no VC affiliation needed for Founders; decisions in ~2–7 days; credits generally expire after ~2 years — [guptadeepak](https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/programs/aws-activate); [stormit](https://www.stormit.cloud/blog/aws-startup-credits/)
- Stripe Atlas perks reportedly include $5,000 AWS Activate credits (Atlas is Delaware C-corp oriented) — [guptadeepak Stripe Atlas](https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/guides/stripe-atlas)

**Google for Startups Cloud Program**
- "Start" tier: for startups that have not received equity funding; up to $2,000 credits valid for one year — [Google startup one-pager (PDF)](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/startup_onepager.pdf)
- Aggregators: apply with matching company email domain; no prior GCP credits beyond free trial; one says founded within last 5 years (unconfirmed by Google); needs working MVP/business model but no investor — [guptadeepak GfS Cloud](https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/guides/google-for-startups-cloud); [builtfirst listing](https://popable.builtfirst.com/google-cloud-cloud-credits-google-workspace)
- Older 2022 Google blog said self-funded credits usable over 2 years (now superseded by 1 year) — [Google Cloud blog 2022](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/startups/updates-and-announcements-startup-summit-2022)
- Scale tiers (up to $200K / AI tier larger) are partner-gated, require equity funding — [fin.ai comparison](https://fin.ai/learn/startup-credit-programs)
- A listing mentions $10K Anthropic (Claude) partner credits via Google Cloud's program/Model Garden (route to Claude credits) — [fin.ai](https://fin.ai/learn/startup-credit-programs)

**Microsoft for Startups (Founders Hub) — CHANGED**
- A June 27, 2025 Microsoft memo ended the old open "up to $150,000" structure for most startups; from July 2025 a two-track system applies, with the largest credits requiring an investor referral — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/microsoft-pulls-program-that-gave-generous-azure-boost-to-ai-startups)
- Reported tiers: self-service up to $5,000 Azure credits; Investor Network up to $150,000 via referral code (secondary source) — [Spendbase](https://spendbase.co/blog/saas-management/azure-credits-for-startups/)
- Mercury perks page: "Incorporated startups new to Azure can get up to $5k in free Azure credits"; more if venture-backed — [Mercury perk: Microsoft for Startups](https://mercury.com/perks/microsoft-for-startups)
- OpenAI credits are no longer available via Founders Hub (Microsoft moderator, May 2025); Azure OpenAI can still be paid with Azure credits — [Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/2275712/is-openai-credits-available-as-part-of-startup-ben)
- Historical: Founders Hub launched 2022 opened to all startups (no VC requirement) — [InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/3653229/microsoft-opens-azure-based-startups-founders-hub-easing-eligibility-rules.html)

**Cloudflare for Startups**
- Official page lists tiers: $5K bootstrapped/stealth, $25K up-and-coming, $100K seed-funded, $250K high-growth (Tier 1 VC/accelerator network); credits generally valid 1 year. Older trackers cite up to $350K (likely outdated) — [Cloudflare for Startups](https://www.cloudflare.com/hatch); [Spendbase](https://www.spendbase.co/blog/cost-optimization/what-are-cloudflare-credits/)

**DigitalOcean**
- A 2026 guide says Hatch is "no longer active" and replaced by the DigitalOcean Startups Program (unverified against DO's page) — [causo.ai guide](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-digitalocean-startups-2026)
- Jan 2025 announcement of new Hatch benefits (up to ~$100K credits figure attributed to that era) — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250122496359/en/DigitalOcean-Announces-New-Benefits-for-Hatch-Startup-Program)
- Historical Hatch eligibility: accelerator/VC affiliation, Series A or less; existing customers ineligible — [Statsig startup tools](https://statsig.com/startuptools/digitalocean-hatch-for-startups)

**AI labs (OpenAI / Anthropic)**
- Anthropic Startup Program: no published credit schedule found; one comparison says credits in practice require institutional equity funding (unconfirmed) — [fin.ai](https://fin.ai/learn/startup-credit-programs); [guptadeepak 2026 map](https://guptadeepak.com/i-mapped-every-major-startup-credit-program-for-2026-most-founders-are-leaving-500k-on-the-table/)
- Brex perks have included $2,500 OpenAI credits (Brex account required) — [guptadeepak Brex](https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/programs/brex-startup)

**SaaS startup programs**
- HubSpot for Startups: 50–90% off HubSpot, generally requires membership in an approved VC/accelerator/incubator partner (e.g., selecting Notion as partner) — [HubSpot x Notion](https://hubspot.com/startups/partners/notion)
- HubSpot startup bundle also lists Reddit $1,000 free ad credits after joining its free program — [HubSpot offers](https://offers.hubspot.com/startups/startup-founders-free-ai-toolkit)
- Notion for Startups (2024 terms): non-paying Notion customer, <50 employees, raised <$10M, partner affiliation; up to 6 months free incl. unlimited AI — [Statsig: Notion for Startups](https://statsig.com/startuptools/notion-for-startups)
- Reddit Ads: $500 free credit when you spend $500 via FounderPass, new advertisers only — [FounderPass Reddit](https://www.founderpass.com/partners/reddit)

### Inferences
- For a non-VC LLC, realistic stackable free cloud value is roughly $1K (AWS) + $2K (GCP) + up to $5K (Azure) + $5K (Cloudflare) ≈ $13K, all requiring a live company website and matching domain email; none require US citizenship per sources found.
- Most SaaS "for Startups" programs (HubSpot, Notion, Intercom, Zendesk, Miro, Airtable) route through partner affiliation; the cheapest path for a bootstrapper is a bank perk marketplace (Mercury/Ramp/Brex) which acts as the "partner."

### Gaps
- Could not verify current official terms for Intercom Early Stage, Zendesk for Startups, Airtable, Miro, Figma, Webflow, Klaviyo, Canva, Semrush/Ahrefs startup deals (no searches completed within budget).
- OpenAI direct startup credit program terms not found.
- Exact current Microsoft self-service amount not confirmed on Microsoft's official page.

## Perk aggregators (Mercury, Brex, Ramp, Stripe Atlas, Secret, Rho, Relay, Wise, Gusto, AppSumo)

### Takeaway
Bank/fintech perk marketplaces are the most accessible source of SaaS credits for a bootstrapped LLC, but access is tied to opening the account (US entity + EIN). Mercury and Ramp are free; Brex reportedly has cash-reserve expectations and is now owned by Capital One (April 2026). Stripe Atlas perks apply only to Atlas-formed companies (Delaware C-corp focused).

### Cited Findings
- Mercury perks: Microsoft up to $5K Azure; Deel $1.5K credits; Google Cloud up to $200K off (likely partner-gated); free-year/free-month deals from Supabase, GitHub, Notion, Linear, Hex, Lovable — [Mercury perks](https://mercury.com/perks)
- Mercury marketplace unlocked by a free business account for US-registered companies — [perkstack (June 2026)](https://perkstack.co/blog/startup-bank-perks)
- Superscout lists Mercury-linked AWS/DigitalOcean cash offers conditional on depositing $50K within 90 days — [superscout Mercury](https://superscout.co/perk/mercury)
- Ramp: free startup tier, "up to $350,000+ in partner perks"; apply at ramp.com/startups; review time conflicts (1–2 vs 12 business days) — [guptadeepak Ramp](https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/programs/ramp-rewards); [rework Ramp vs Brex](https://resources.rework.com/tools/spend-expense/ramp-vs-brex)
- Brex Startup: free for incorporated US entity with EIN, typically $50K+ cash reserves; perks under Dashboard > Rewards; Capital One completed $5.15B acquisition of Brex on April 7, 2026 — [guptadeepak Brex](https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/programs/brex-startup); [businessinitiative](https://www.businessinitiative.org/business-credit-cards/brex-card/)
- Stripe Atlas: $500 one-time (Delaware C-corp, EIN for non-US founders, 83(b) help); perks reportedly up to $2,500 Stripe processing credits (12 months) + $50K+ partner perks incl. $5K AWS; post-year-1 costs ~$300 DE franchise tax min + ~$100 registered agent — [guptadeepak Atlas](https://guptadeepak.com/startup-offers/guides/stripe-atlas); [unilink Atlas 2026](https://unil.ink/help-center/articles/stripe-atlas-2026)

### Inferences
- An LLC owner already banking with Mercury (common for foreign-owned LLCs) gets the marketplace at no cost; Ramp can be added for a second marketplace if approved.
- Stripe Atlas perks are not available to an existing non-Atlas LLC.

### Gaps
- No results found on Secret (joinsecret.com) pricing/free tier, Rho, Relay, Wise, Gusto perks, or AppSumo; report writer should note these as unverified.
- Whether Ramp/Brex approve foreign-owned single-member LLCs was not confirmed.

## Business-only accounts & discounts (Amazon Business, Costco/Sam's, Apple, Dell/Lenovo, Workspace, M365, Adobe, Shopify, email tools)

### Takeaway
Amazon Business offers the Amazon Tax Exemption Program (ATEP), letting a reseller apply state resale certificates at checkout — useful for e-commerce inventory/packaging bought for resale. Other business-pricing programs could not be verified within research budget.

### Cited Findings
- Amazon Business ATEP: apply resale exemption at checkout; requires a resale certificate (or similar) for each jurisdiction shipped to; rules differ by state — [Amazon Business blog](https://business.amazon.com/es/discover-more/blog/what-is-wholesale); [sellersonar](https://sellersonar.com/blog/tax-exemption-amazon/)
- ATEP enrollment via Tax Exemption Wizard (~15 min) or manual upload (up to 24h); covers Amazon/affiliate items and opted-in third-party sellers only — [sagemailer](https://sagemailer.com/blog/tax-exemption-amazon/)
- Historical (2016) limitation: not usable for machine & equipment exemptions — [UW newsletter](https://finance.uw.edu/fm/ps/sites/default/files/news/campus-newsletter/2016%20PS%20Campus%20Newsletter%201-28.pdf)

### Inferences
- Resale certificates only exempt items bought for resale (inventory), not office supplies; misuse creates sales tax liability.

### Gaps
- Not verified: Amazon Business account fees / Business Prime tiers, Costco Business / Sam's Club, Apple business pricing, Dell/Lenovo small business, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 Business pricing, Adobe, Shopify plans, Klaviyo, ConvertKit/Kit.

## Developer/organization accounts needing D-U-N-S (Apple, Google Play, Meta, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, X)

### Takeaway
A US LLC can enroll as an organization in Apple Developer ($99/yr) and Google Play ($25 one-time) with a free D-U-N-S number; the org Google Play account notably avoids the 12-tester/14-day closed test required of new personal accounts. Apple fee waivers are only for nonprofits/education/government, not LLCs.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Developer Program: $99/year; fee waiver only for nonprofits, accredited educational institutions, government entities; organization must be a legal entity (no DBAs/trade names) — [Apple enroll page](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/?product=WM); [TechCrunch 2018](https://techcrunch.com/2018/01/03/apple-developer-program-fee-waivers-are-now-available-for-nonprofits-schools-and-government/)
- Forum reports of US LLC org enrollment declined after D-U-N-S verification (data must match exactly) — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/840067)
- Google Play org account: D-U-N-S required, $25 registration fee — [capgo](https://capgo.app/blog/how-to-create-apple-developer-and-google-play-developer-accounts/)
- Personal Play accounts created after Nov 13, 2023 must run a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days per app; organization accounts are reportedly exempt — [testerscommunity](https://www.testerscommunity.com/guides/how-many-testers-do-you-need-google-play); [ontest](https://ontest.app/blog/personal-vs-organization-google-play-account-12-testers)
- D-U-N-S is free; timing reported ~5 business days to up to 30 days — [capgo](https://capgo.app/blog/how-to-create-apple-developer-and-google-play-developer-accounts/); [uscreen](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/10289133-choosing-the-right-developer-account-for-your-app-individual-vs-organizational)

### Inferences
- Get the D-U-N-S early (free via Apple's lookup tool) since it unblocks Apple, Google Play, and often Meta business verification.

### Gaps
- Meta Business verification, Google Business Profile (requires real address for verification), LinkedIn company page, X verified organizations pricing — not researched within budget.

## Grants, competitions and SBA loans for small/foreign-owned LLCs

### Takeaway
SBA loans are closed to this LLC: since March 1, 2026, 100% of owners must be US citizens/nationals residing in the US (even green card holders excluded). Hello Alice grants are limited to US residents/businesses; Amber Grant criteria focus on a US (or US/Canada) business that is 50%+ women-owned, with no citizenship requirement found, but residency is likely checked in each grant's official rules.

### Cited Findings
- SBA (effective March 1, 2026): 100% of direct/indirect owners must be US Citizens or US Nationals with principal residence in the US; LPRs excluded; also applies to microloans and Surety Bond Guarantee — [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/loans/news/sba-loan-green-card-holders); [Dickinson Wright](https://www.dickinson-wright.com/news-alerts/client-alert-sba-loan-restrictions-for-noncitizens)
- History: pre-2025 51% citizen/LPR "majority rule"; June 2025 SOP required 100% citizen/national/LPR; Dec 19, 2025 notice allowed up to 5% noncitizen ownership, rescinded Feb 2026 — [Dickinson Wright](https://www.dickinson-wright.com/news-alerts/client-alert-sba-loan-restrictions-for-noncitizens); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/sba-loans-are-now-exclusively-for-u-s-citizens-nationals/)
- Hello Alice: eligibility varies by grant; FAQ versions say grants open only to legal residents of the 50 states + DC / "U.S. residents" — [Hello Alice support](https://support.helloalice.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406961806619-Are-grants-hosted-on-Hello-Alice-open-to-businesses-outside-of-the-U-S); [Hello Alice Notion FAQ](https://helloalice.notion.site/Do-you-accept-applications-from-businesses-located-outside-of-the-United-States-8b6d7e0166ea41c1accff6d4e2b9632e)
- Amber Grant: 50%+ women-owned; applicant 18+; business based in US or Canada per some sources, US only per others (conflict) — [Ambrook](https://ambrook.com/funding/amber-grant-for-women); [LAEDC](https://laedc.org/?p=57531)

### Inferences
- "US resident" requirements in most private grants likely refer to the applicant/owner, which would exclude a non-resident owner even if the LLC is US-registered; must check each program's official rules.

### Gaps
- FedEx Small Business Grant and Etsy Uplift Fund current status/eligibility not verified.

## Business insurance for e-commerce (Amazon/Faire requirements; Next, Hiscox, Thimble, Simply Business)

### Takeaway
Amazon requires $1M per-occurrence/aggregate commercial liability insurance once gross monthly sales exceed $10K, and from November 2, 2026 also for listings in enhanced-safety categories (children's, cosmetics/ingestibles, lithium batteries) regardless of sales. Foreign-owned LLCs often get declined by US insurers if a foreign address is given; NEXT is the carrier in Amazon's Insurance Accelerator.

### Cited Findings
- $10K/month gross sales trigger; obtain within 30 days; $1M per occurrence and aggregate; Amazon named additional insured — [EcommerceBytes](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/me7y); [SellerSprite](https://www.sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-seller-insurance-requirements)
- Effective Nov 2, 2026: category-based trigger added (children's, cosmetic/ingestible, lithium battery products), regardless of $10K threshold (secondary coverage of Amazon announcement) — [beBOLD](https://www.bebolddigital.com/news/amazon-liability-insurance-requirements-november-2026)
- Non-resident sellers: "most US insurance companies will decline your application if you provide a foreign address"; workaround is US EIN + US address; some non-residents' policies canceled and reported to Amazon — [NVinc](https://nvinc.com/amazon-seller-insurance-requirements-for-non-resident-sellers/)
- NEXT is the US carrier offering policies via Amazon Insurance Accelerator (undated) — [Digital Insurance](https://dig-in.com/news/amazon-marsh-next-small-business-insurance-accelerator); Next lists Amazon Seller coverage, subject to underwriting/state availability — [Next Insurance](https://www.nextinsurance.com/a/answers/business-insurance/does-amazon-seller-business-insurance-cover-loss-of-inventory)

### Gaps
- Hiscox, Thimble, Simply Business underwriting policy on non-resident owners not confirmed; Faire insurance requirements not researched.

## US phone numbers, virtual addresses, registered agents

### Takeaway
Quo (formerly OpenPhone, rebranded late 2025) offers US/Canada numbers from ~$15/user/mo annual; A2P 10DLC registration with an EIN is needed to text US numbers.

### Cited Findings
- OpenPhone became Quo in late 2025 (raised $105M) — [Quo blog](https://www.quo.com/blog/phone-com-alternatives/)
- Plans Starter/Business/Scale: $15/$23/$35 annual, $19/$33/$47 monthly per user; 1 number per user, extra numbers $5/mo; US/Canada numbers only — [ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/openphone-pricing); [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/quo-pricing/)
- A2P registration (Business with EIN) ~$19.50 one-time + $1.50/mo; cannot text US numbers until approved — [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/quo-pricing/)

### Gaps
- Google Voice (Workspace), Grasshopper, Dialpad, virtual address and registered-agent perks not researched; whether Quo accepts non-US-resident signups not confirmed.
