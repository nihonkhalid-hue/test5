# Digital Products Master List: Anime, Manga, Gaming, Retro & Japanese Pop Culture First

Research date: October 2026. Sources: Etsy, itch.io, Booth.pm, Gumroad, Fab/Unreal, Unity Asset Store, Superhive, Elgato Marketplace, DriveThruRPG, Minecraft Marketplace, Cults3D/MyMiniFactory, GoodNotes Marketplace, DLsite/Fanbox/Fantia, and creator storefronts. The full source list is at the bottom.

**How to read this list**
- The list is ordered by niche priority: anime, manga, gaming, retro, J-pop culture first, then everything else.
- ⭐ means strong evidence of demand: many sellers, review counts, or reported revenue.
- 💡 means a gap or under-served angle found during research.
- ⚠️ IP means the product only works with **original** characters/designs or an official license. Etsy removes listings on DMCA notices and can close shops after repeated notices. An "anime style" can't be owned, but specific characters, names and logos can.

## IP rules to keep in mind
- Sell **original** characters and designs. The anime style is free to use. Specific characters, names, logos and official art are not.
- Don't put franchise names in titles or tags unless you have a license.
- Retro: don't sell ROMs, BIOS files or patched copies of commercial games. Original homebrew, tools, guides and art are fine.
- Fonts, music and base models each have their own license. Check "commercial use" and "resale" before bundling.
