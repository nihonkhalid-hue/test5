# File by October 15, then unlock the rest

The most valuable thing this US LLC can do for its Moroccan owner this week is avoid a penalty. Every foreign-owned single-member LLC must file a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472 attached for every year it exists, even with zero activity. The penalty for missing it is **$25,000 per form per year**, and the extended deadline for tax year 2025 is **October 15, 2026**, five days from now ([Taxes for Expats](https://www.taxesforexpats.com/articles/foreign-business/form-5472-penalty.html)). After that, the second urgent problem is shipping. Since **July 9, 2026 Etsy requires non-US sellers to ship DDP to US buyers** ([Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-requires-ddp-shipping-us-tariffs/)). De minimis is gone for every country, and Moroccan goods now carry a **12.5% Section 301 tariff** on top of normal duty ([AACB](https://www.aacb.com/trade-tariff-news/section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-on-60-economies)). Once those are handled, the LLC opens five doors that have real value: an ITIN-backed identity (Shopify Payments, PayPal Working Capital, Amex), Mercury's balance-underwritten IO card and perks marketplace, Faire Direct at 0% commission, Walmart Marketplace (which Morocco-based sellers cannot join without a US entity), and correct US-Morocco FTA claims that remove the base (MFN) duty. The LLC's "0% US tax" status is real only while nothing is in the US: no inventory, staff or agent there. Morocco taxes the profits anyway, at up to 37%, and Office des Changes rules on foreign accounts and repatriation are the risk that US formation vendors almost never mention ([IGOC 2024](https://www.oc.gov.ma/sites/default/files/reglementation/pdf/2024-01/IGOC%202024.pdf)).

> **How to read this report.** This is not legal or tax advice. Confirm every filing decision with a US CPA who handles 5472 returns, and with a Moroccan fiduciary for exchange control and Moroccan tax. **No Reddit threads could be retrieved**: searches returned none and direct fetches were blocked. Any "community wisdom" below therefore comes from practitioner blogs, vendor sites, and the Shopify, Apple and Amazon forums, never from Reddit. Most sites (irs.gov, mercury.com, stripe.com, faire.com, amazon.com) could not be opened directly, so **many figures come from search-result summaries** of the cited pages. Many sources are LLC-formation or fintech vendors with a commercial interest. Check every number on the official page before acting on it.

## Five things to fix in the next 30 days

**Form 5472 and pro forma 1120 (deadline Oct 15, 2026 if extended).** An LLC about 1.5 years old was probably formed in spring 2025. Its first return, for tax year 2025, was due April 15, 2026, or October 15, 2026 if a Form 7004 extension was filed by April 15 ([Kozlaw](https://kozlaw.com/april-15-deadline-u-s-form-5472-for-foreign-owned-single-member-llc/)). If the LLC was formed in late 2024, a 2024 return was also due. These returns **cannot be e-filed**. Secondary sources give fax 855-887-7737 or mail to the IRS in Ogden, "Attn: PIN Unit". This was not verified against the IRS instructions, so confirm before sending, and keep the fax confirmation ([Globalfy](https://globalfy.com/blog/form-5472/); [Greenback](https://www.greenbacktaxservices.com/knowledge-center/form-5472/)). Reportable transactions are the owner's contributions, draws and LLC-paid personal expenses, so a clean Mercury ledger matters ([Nonresident Tax](https://nonresident.tax/blog/form-5472-pro-forma-1120-guide-nonresident-owners/)). If no extension was filed, the return is already late. Practitioners file at once with a reasonable-cause statement and do not wait for a notice. Sources disagree on whether first-time abatement applies to 5472 penalties. If a penalty is assessed, request relief with Form 843 rather than simply paying ([SDO CPA](https://www.sdocpa.com/form-5472-penalties-relief-guide/); [Optimize Tax](https://optimizetax.io/blog/form-5472-penalty-relief/)). Do not count on the Farhy/Mukhi litigation to escape these penalties. The D.C. Circuit sided with the IRS, and those cases concerned a different code section ([Marcum](https://marcumllp.com/insights/dc-circuit-lets-stand-its-decision-allowing-irs-to-assess-international-information-return-penalties)). A standalone CPA typically charges **$500–$2,000 a year**. Doola's compliance tier costs $1,999 a year and Firstbase's filing add-on $1,799 ([GlobalSolo](https://www.globalsolo.global/data/llc-costs-by-state)).

**W-8BEN, not W-9, on every platform.** A single-member LLC owned by a foreigner is disregarded for US tax. IRS instructions say the foreign single owner provides a **Form W-8BEN** and tells the payer the account is held in the disregarded entity's name ([IRS W-8BEN-E instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/iw8bene.pdf); [IRS W-8BEN instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben)). Stripe, Amazon, Apple and Walmart flows sometimes push foreign-owned LLCs toward a W-9. Practitioners call that a potential false certification of US-person status ([Riverbend Consulting](https://riverbendconsulting.com/blog/amazon-tax-interview/); [Apple Dev Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/834406)). The practical harm is that a W-9 leads to 1099-K and 1099 reporting on a "US person" that files no US income return. That mismatch is a natural trigger for IRS notices. Audit Stripe, PayPal, Etsy, Amazon Associates and any ad network, and resubmit wherever a W-9 was given. For treaty claims, the W-8BEN can carry the owner's Moroccan tax ID or an ITIN. The LLC's EIN is not the owner's TIN for this purpose ([GlobalSolo](https://www.globalsolo.global/blog/w-9-vs-w-8ben-vs-w-8ben-e-foreign-owned-single-member-llc-2026.md)). A useful side fact: OBBBA restored the 1099-K threshold to **more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions** ([Stripe support](https://support.stripe.com/questions/whats-new-for-the-2026-tax-season)).

**Etsy DDP, de minimis and the 12.5% tariff.** The $800 duty-free de minimis was suspended for all countries on Aug 29, 2025. CBP made the suspension indefinite on June 24, 2026, and statutory repeal follows on July 1, 2027 ([Federal Register 2026-12670](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12670/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-merchandise-arriving-through-all-modes-other)). The Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA "reciprocal" tariffs on Feb 20, 2026 ([Flexport](https://www.flexport.com/blog/the-supreme-courts-ieepa-tariff-ruling-next-steps-potential-refunds-and/)). Since July 24, 2026, Morocco has been in the **12.5% Section 301 forced-labor tier (HTS 9903.05.56)**, stacked on MFN duty ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/07/and-the-tariff-beat-goes-on)). Etsy's rule: non-DDP orders to the US lose Purchase Protection, and any duties charged to the buyer are refunded and deducted from the seller. Etsy has a beta tariff estimator in the listing editor and names UPS and FedEx as DDP-capable ([Etsy community announcement](https://community.etsy.com/forum/announcements-290/topic/upcoming-policy-changes-for-orders-shipped-to-the-us-183836/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-tests-us-tariff-calculator/)). DHL's 2026 US guide prices its duty-tax-paid service at **2% of duties, minimum $17** ([DHL rate guide](https://www.dhl.com/content/dam/dhl/global/dhl-express/documents/pdf/service-and-rate-guide-us-en.pdf)). Reprice every Etsy and madeinatlas.com listing so that duty is built in. Whether Amana/EMS can currently move US-bound parcels with duties collected could not be confirmed, so do not rely on it.

**Morocco: worldwide tax and Office des Changes.** A Moroccan tax resident (more than 183 days in Morocco) is taxed on worldwide income at progressive rates of **0–37%**. Because the LLC is disregarded, its profits are the owner's income. US tax paid is $0, so there is no foreign tax credit to offset Moroccan tax ([Neo Expertise](https://neoexpertise.net/tax-morocco-foreign-investors/); [creer-llc-usa.com](https://creer-llc-usa.com/articles/fr/llc-usa-maroc-2026)). Under the 2024 General Instruction, Moroccan residents need authorization to hold accounts or assets abroad unless a general authorization applies ([IGOC 2024](https://www.oc.gov.ma/sites/default/files/reglementation/pdf/2024-01/IGOC%202024.pdf)). Pay for **online services** rendered from Morocco must be **repatriated within 90 days** ([La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/services-rendus-en-ligne-le-rapatriement-des-remunerations-doit-etre-realise-dans-un-delai-de-90-jours/)). That rule plainly reaches retromemory.com's ad and affiliate income. How it applies to physical exports sold through a foreign LLC was not established in this research. A Mercury balance is technically a foreign account held by a Moroccan resident. One firm also reports that Finance Law 2026 added an annual declaration of foreign-source income, due March 1 or April 1 (sources conflict) ([CMS](https://cms.law/fr/mar/news-information/obligations-declaratives-pour-les-residents-fiscaux-du-maroc)). This is the single largest unaddressed risk in the structure. Book a Moroccan fiduciary before year-end, repatriate on a regular schedule, and label every transfer as an owner distribution.

**Housekeeping that silently kills accounts.** Mercury does not accept a registered-agent, PO box or UPS Store address as the principal place of business. A residential address, including one in Morocco, is fine. Third-party guides link registered-agent addresses to later account closures ([Mercury Support](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28769699533588-Company-address-requirements); [ExpatMemo](https://expatmemo.com/mercury-account-outside-us/)). Mismatched legal name, address, website and bank details are the most common reason Stripe stalls or freezes foreign accounts ([James Baker CPA](https://jamesbakercpa.com/blog/stripe-account-frozen-what-to-do/)). Keep the state annual report current: Wyoming $60, New Mexico $0, Delaware $300 (some tables say $400), Florida $138.75. Administrative dissolution cuts off banking and Stripe ([LLC University](https://www.llcuniversity.com/llc-annual-fees-by-state/)). One item can be crossed off. Since FinCEN's rule, finalized effective **Aug 14, 2026**, a US-formed LLC owes **no BOI report**, whatever the owner's nationality. Ignore any vendor upsell for one ([Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/09/FinCEN-Permanently-Lifts-Certain-Beneficial-Ownership-Reporting-Requirements)).

## Eight unlocks that repay the effort fastest

**1. An ITIN is the master key, but it has a gate.** Shopify Payments US and Shopify Balance officially require an SSN or ITIN. So does PayPal Working Capital, which asks for owner SSNs. Without an ITIN, Shop Pay, Shopify Collective and Shopify's lower transaction fees stay out of reach ([Shopify Help: Balance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/finance/shopify-balance/eligibility); [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/shopify-payments-for-foreign-owned-us-llc/306063); [PayPal Working Capital](https://www.paypal.com/us/webapps/mpp/paypal-working-capital)). An ITIN also opens bank business cards. One vendor says "seven of the ten largest US card issuers accept an ITIN", with the caveat that "accepted does not mean approved" ([founderscredit](https://founderscredit.llc/blog/us-amex-for-non-residents)). A Certifying Acceptance Agent costs **$150–$400** and avoids mailing the passport. Processing takes **7–14 weeks** ([Jupid](https://jupid.com/blog/what-is-an-itin-guide-2026); [Terms.law](https://terms.law/Invest-USA/entity-formation/itin-application.html)). The catch is that a W-7 must be tied to a qualifying tax reason, usually a return, so a standalone application without an exception is typically rejected ([Dimov Tax](https://dimovtax.com/form-w-7-itin-application/)). Ask the CPA preparing the 5472 whether an exception fits, for example a treaty claim on withheld income such as YouTube or KDP royalties.

**2. Mercury IO and the perks marketplace (already paid for).** IO pays **1.5% unlimited cashback** with no annual fee. Third-party guides say it is underwritten on account balance, not SSN or credit score. Keeping **$15,000+** across Mercury accounts converts daily autopay into **30-day terms** ([Mercury](https://mercury.com/credit); [Rho blog](https://www.rho.co/blog/mercury-business-credit-card-reviews); [GlobalSolo](https://www.globalsolo.global/blog/mercury-card-vs-ramp-vs-brex-vs-rho-corporate-cards-2026)). An aggregator counted **272 Mercury perks** in October 2026, including up to $5k in Azure credits and Gusto promos ([startupperks.co](https://startupperks.co/partner-perks/mercury); [Mercury Perks](https://mercury.com/perks)). Treasury yield figures conflict: "up to 4.00%" on Mercury's page versus a reported $250k minimum. It matters only at larger balances ([Rho vs Mercury](https://www.rho.co/versus/mercury)). Whether Morocco is on Mercury's restricted-residence list could not be confirmed. In 2024 Mercury offboarded founders based on where they lived, so a backup bank is cheap insurance ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/23/mercury-bank-fintech-sanctions-ukraine-nigeria)).

**3. Faire Direct at 0% commission, and possibly skipping the 10% foreign surcharge.** Faire charges **25% on first orders from new marketplace retailers, 15% on reorders, and 0% on orders through your Faire Direct link**. Brands **outside North America pay an extra one-time 10% referral fee** on a new retailer's first order ([Faire Help](https://faire.com/support/articles/360040446591); [Faire PDF](https://cdn.faire.com/static/asset/What%20is%20Faire%20%28M2M%29.pdf)). Faire Direct also gives your own boutique and interior-designer contacts **net-60 terms** with Faire carrying the credit risk. Moving existing wholesale contacts onto Faire Direct is the highest-margin channel available. Whether a US LLC with a US bank qualifies as "North American" and so avoids the 10% fee is plausible but unconfirmed. Ask Faire directly.

**4. The US-Morocco FTA claim cuts the base duty to zero.** The importer claims at entry by prefixing the HTS code with **SPI "MA"**. A missed claim can be corrected by post-summary correction within one year, but post-importation 1520(d) claims are not available ([19 CFR 10.762](https://www.gpo.gov/fdsys/pkg/CFR-2014-title19-vol1/pdf/CFR-2014-title19-vol1-sec10-762.pdf); [CBP Morocco FTA FAQ](https://cbp.gov/trade/free-trade-agreements/morocco/faqs)). The only source to address it says the FTA does **not** exempt goods from the 12.5% Section 301 duty ([Zonos](https://zonos.com/docs/guides/country-guides/united-states/section-301-forced-labor)). Take a $500 hand-knotted wool rug (HTS 5701.10.90, MFN 4.5%) ([USITC HTS ch. 57](https://usitc.gov/publications/docs/tata/hts/bychapter/0500c57.pdf)). With a correct FTA claim it pays about $62.50 in duty. Without one it pays about $85. The saving is larger on leather and ceramic lines with higher MFN rates; look those up on hts.usitc.gov. Express couriers clearing informal entries may not claim the FTA by default. Print "Origin: Morocco, claim US-Morocco FTA (SPI MA)" plus the HTS code on every commercial invoice, and confirm the broker applies it. This is an inference from how claims work, not verified carrier practice.

**5. Walmart Marketplace is effectively LLC-only for Moroccans.** Walmart rejects sole proprietors. Morocco was not on its 2024 list of countries whose sellers may join without a US entity ([M2E Pro](https://blog.m2epro.com/can-i-sell-on-the-walmart-marketplace-from-europe/)). Walmart also requires a US phone, a physical US return address, and GTIN/UPCs ([EcomClips](https://ecomclips.com/blog/walmart-marketplace-requirements/)). It is a phase-two channel after US 3PL stock exists. Walmart reportedly prefers W-9 filers, which conflicts with the W-8BEN rule above, so settle the tax step with the CPA first ([nvinc](https://nvinc.com/which-entity-is-best-to-sell-on-walmart/)).

**6. Amazon Handmade plus a USPTO trademark.** Handmade charges a **15% referral fee**. The **$39.99 Professional fee is waived from month two** for approved artisans, and there are no listing fees ([BeBold Digital](https://www.bebolddigital.com/blog/sell-on-amazon-handmade); [Amazon.ca Handmade](https://sell.amazon.ca/programs/handmade)). Brand Registry accepts registered or pending USPTO marks and unlocks A+ content, Brand Store and copycat enforcement ([SellerLabs](https://www.sellerlabs.com/blog/amazon-brand-registry-requirements-2026/)). That enforcement is valuable in knockoff-heavy categories like poufs and rugs. USPTO filing costs **$350 per class**. A Morocco-domiciled applicant must use a US-licensed attorney, and the LLC does not avoid this if its headquarters is in Morocco ([Fish & Richardson](https://www.fr.com/insights/thought-leadership/blogs/new-uspto-trademark-fees-go-into-effect-january-18-2025/); [Hahn Law](https://www.hahnlaw.com/insights/uspto-requires-us-licensed-attorney-for-foreign-trademark-applicants-and-registrants/)). Amazon requires $1M liability insurance above $10k a month in sales. From Nov 2, 2026 it is also required in certain categories regardless of sales. US insurers often decline foreign addresses ([EcommerceBytes](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/me7y); [beBOLD](https://www.bebolddigital.com/news/amazon-liability-insurance-requirements-november-2026); [NVinc](https://nvinc.com/amazon-seller-insurance-requirements-for-non-resident-sellers/)).

**7. A USD Etsy shop avoids the 2.5% conversion fee, at the cost of your reviews.** Etsy charges **2.5% currency conversion** when listing currency differs from the payout account currency ([Checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees)). On a $2,000 rug that is $50. But Etsy cannot change a shop's bank country, so switching to Mercury means opening a new shop ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015710408)). A sensible test is a second LLC shop for one category. Separately, Offsite Ads take 12–15%, capped at $100 per order, which is a reason to move repeat rug buyers to madeinatlas.com ([Crosslist](https://crosslist.com/blog/etsy-review)).

**8. Bulk import through the LLC as importer of record, with eyes open.** With de minimis gone, every parcel carries an entry and brokerage fees. One formal entry per pallet, with the LLC filing CBP Form 5106 under its EIN, spreads those fees across many items. Domestic sales then ship from a US 3PL with no per-parcel duty ([Greenwich Mercantile](https://www.greenwich-mercantile.com/resources/guides/cbp-form-5106)). Three costs offset this. First, inventory in a US warehouse is the main gray zone for a **US trade or business**, which could end the "0% US tax" position and require a 1040-NR with a treaty disclosure ([James Baker CPA](https://jamesbakercpa.com/learn/ecommerce-sellers/)). Second, the inventory creates **physical sales-tax nexus** in the warehouse state. Third, EO 14411 (June 2026) will tighten bond and vetting rules for foreign-controlled importers once CBP writes the rules ([Carra Globe](https://carraglobe.com/customs-enforcement-executive-order-ior-2026/)). ShipMonk's minimum is about $250 a month. ShipBob's is about $275 plus roughly $975 setup ([FitSmallBusiness](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/shipbob-vs-shipmonk/); [Delivery Group](https://deliverygroupinc.com/blog/shipbob-pricing-2026)). Model the numbers with a CPA before moving stock.

### Tax and compliance: the recurring calendar

The federal position is simple as long as everything stays in Morocco. With no US office, staff, dependent agent or inventory, practitioners treat the owner as **not engaged in a US trade or business**. That means no US income tax and no 1040-NR, only the annual 5472/1120 ([Neo Expertise](https://neoexpertise.net/tax-morocco-foreign-investors/)). The 1977 US-Morocco treaty, in force since 1981, would protect business profits without a US permanent establishment. Its exact storage and delivery exclusions could not be read in this research ([IRS treaty PDF](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/morocco.pdf)). Keep any US virtual assistant independent, non-exclusive and without authority to sign contracts, so they cannot become a "dependent agent" ([Entity.inc](https://www.entity.inc/blog/effectively-connected-income-eci/)).

Sales tax is a state-by-state threshold question. Most states use $100k in sales. California and Texas use $500k, and New York uses $500k plus 100 sales. More than 17 states have dropped the 200-transaction test, including Illinois (Jan 2026) and Kentucky (Aug 2026) ([VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/10/02/states-drop-200-transaction-sales-tax-nexus-thresholds/); [Kintsugi](https://trykintsugi.com/blog/how-to-determine-nexus-in-every-state)). Etsy and Amazon collect tax on marketplace sales. For own-site sales, **Stripe Tax's threshold monitoring is free**, and calculation costs 0.5% only where you are registered ([Stripe Tax pricing](https://stripe.com/en-gr/tax/pricing)). Amazon Business's tax-exemption program (ATEP) applies resale certificates at checkout, but only to goods bought for resale ([sellersonar](https://sellersonar.com/blog/tax-exemption-amazon/)).

Pitfalls, ranked by cost:

1. Missing a 5472.
2. Giving a W-9.
3. Assuming "0% US tax" means 0% tax, and ignoring Morocco.
4. Moving stock to the US without re-running the trade-or-business, nexus and treaty analysis.
5. Letting the registered agent or annual report lapse. The agent's address is also where IRS penalty notices arrive.
6. Paying for a BOI filing that is no longer required.

The "$50,000+ accumulated penalties" scenarios on vendor sites are hypothetical calculations, not documented cases ([GlobalSolo](https://www.globalsolo.global/blog/what-happens-if-you-miss-form-5472-non-resident-llc)).

## Closed doors and false shortcuts

Several widely promoted "LLC benefits" do not apply to this owner. **SBA loans and microloans** have required 100% US-citizen or US-national owners living in the US since March 1, 2026 ([Dickinson Wright](https://www.dickinson-wright.com/news-alerts/client-alert-sba-loan-restrictions-for-noncitizens)). Most private grants check the owner's residence, not the LLC's state ([Hello Alice](https://helloalice.notion.site/Do-you-accept-applications-from-businesses-located-outside-of-the-United-States-8b6d7e0166ea41c1accff6d4e2b9632e)). Creator funds gate on where the creator lives, and faking US residency through an LLC or VPN breaks their terms ([shortsync](https://www.shortsync.app/resources/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026)). The "use the W-9 with the EIN" shortcut misstates US-person status. Only electing C-corp treatment with Form 8832 makes a W-9 legitimate, and that brings 21% federal corporate tax plus 15% treaty withholding on dividends, which rarely beats the current setup for a small business ([IRS Morocco explanation](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/morocco-treasury-explanation)). Stripe Atlas perks belong only to Atlas-formed companies. A Fair Trade Federation membership is closed to Morocco-based producers ([FTF](https://fairtradefederation.org/?p=487)). A registered-agent address can harm the business wherever a real address is required.

## Conclusion

The LLC's value for madeinatlas.com has shifted since de minimis ended. It used to be a payment workaround. Now it is the legal vehicle for **wholesale (Faire Direct), US marketplaces (Walmart, Amazon Handmade) and, eventually, bulk importing with FTA claims**. Each of those, however, pulls the business closer to the US trade-or-business line that currently keeps US tax at zero. The real decision for 2027 is whether margin gains from US-held stock outweigh the cost of a 1040-NR treaty position, state sales-tax registration and tighter importer vetting. That needs a numbers-based conversation with a CPA, not a vendor checklist.

The asymmetry is also worth naming. US formation vendors emphasize the US side, where obligations are cheap and penalties are large and certain. The Moroccan side gets far less attention, though it holds the actual tax bill and the exchange-control exposure. A Moroccan fiduciary engagement is therefore the most underpriced item on this list. For retromemory.com, the LLC simply serves as a USD bank account: it opens payouts that Morocco cannot receive directly, and growing Tier-1 traffic will do more for revenue than any part of the entity structure.
