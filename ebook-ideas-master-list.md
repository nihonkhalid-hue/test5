# Ebook Ideas Master List: Japan, Anime, Manga, Gaming, Retro + the Best Niches Outside That World

Research date: October 2026. Companion to `digital-products-master-list.md`.

**Legend**
- ⭐ = proven demand (bestseller lists, long-running sellers, crowdfunding results)
- 💡 = gap / under-served angle
- 📈 = rising category on Amazon non-fiction (momentum data from a 61,879-book analysis, Inkfluence 2026)
- ⚠️ = copyright/trademark care needed (no official art, logos or character names in the cover/title without a license)

**Formats you can sell for each idea:** Kindle ebook (KDP), paperback/hardcover (KDP print), PDF on Gumroad/Payhip/Ko-fi/Etsy, EPUB on Kobo/Apple/Google Play, Booth (Japanese buyers), audiobook (ACX/Findaway), and serial chapters on Patreon/Royal Road.

## Rules & risks to know before publishing
- **KDP AI policy:** you must disclose AI-*generated* text, images or translations (AI-*assisted* editing doesn't need it). Reported enforcement tightened in 2026, with undisclosed AI titles pulled and repeat offenders suspended. KDP also caps new titles per account per day (guides report 3/day; Amazon hasn't published the exact number).
- **Game guides for new releases:** AI "guideslop" floods (e.g. Fire Emblem on Amazon Japan) have made buyers wary. Write deep, tested guides, label them "unofficial", and don't use official logos/box art.
- **Anime/manga/game IP:** titles, characters and logos are trademarked/copyrighted. Commentary, history and analysis are OK; official art on covers and character names in coloring/fiction titles are not.
- **Go narrow:** generic "mental health journal" or "weight loss" is saturated; "ADHD time management workbook for college students" is the kind of title that wins.
- **Series beat singles:** one-console-per-book, one-JLPT-level-per-book, one-city-per-guide all build a catalogue that cross-sells.
- **Use Kindle Unlimited for fiction** (LitRPG, light novels), direct sales (Gumroad/Payhip PDF + bonus files like Anki decks, audio, printables) for non-fiction where you can charge more.
